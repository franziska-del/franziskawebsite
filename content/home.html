Another hack, another morning ritual, another habit to squeeze into an already full day. So much advice for living better gives us more to do, more to become and more ways to feel like we’re falling behind.

Dangerously Alive begins with a different idea: aliveness comes from subtraction, not addition.

Unlearn the rules you never chose. Unhook from the need for approval. Loosen your grip on how everything should unfold. Uncover the curiosity, courage and connection that get buried when life becomes something to get on top of.

Through stories from the road, honest reflections and a few dangerous questions, my new book invites you to discover what you can let go of, so you can engage more fully with being here.

Hi, I’m Franziska or Frankie as my friends like to call me.

I’m a Swiss-born Australian (or Swaussie) entrepreneur, adventurer, author and speaker.

I’ve built businesses, written books, ridden a motorcycle along the Silk Road and spent a considerable amount of time wondering why we humans do the things we do.

Across all of it, I keep coming back to a few questions. What becomes possible when we’re a little braver? What are we missing when we stop being curious? And how can we contribute something useful while enjoying our time here?

These questions find their way into my books, my businesses, my talks and probably far too many dinner conversations.

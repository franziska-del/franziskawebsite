Aliveness comes from subtraction, not addition.

Another hack, another morning ritual, another habit to squeeze into an already full day. So much advice for living better gives us more to do, more to become and more ways to feel like we’re falling behind.

Unlearn the rules you never chose. Unhook from the need for approval. Loosen your grip on how everything should unfold. Uncover the curiosity, courage and connection that get buried when life becomes something to get on top of.

Through stories from the road, honest reflections and a few dangerous questions, my new book invites you to discover what you can let go of, so you can engage more fully with being here.

Come with an open mind. Bring your sense of humour.
